# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-31

Initial release.

- Visitor widget (Shadow DOM, reference-based design), pre-chat/offline forms, files, voice notes, KB, CSAT, transcripts.
- Telegram Reply Mode and Topics Mode with exact message/thread mappings and per-operator authorization.
- Durable outbox and inbound update log with leases, retries, uncertain-outcome holding and dead letters.
- Inbox, assignment (manual, round robin, least busy), teams, notes, tags, canned replies.
- Targeting/automation rules with shared PHP/TypeScript evaluation.
- Reports, privacy exporters/erasers, retention, diagnostics, WP-CLI commands.
- English source strings, POT, and a Persian (fa_IR) translation of visitor-facing strings.
