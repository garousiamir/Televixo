Our story · Since 2018

Clothesmade to be kept, not replaced

Chio started in a small Lisbon studio with one idea: that a wardrobe should feel effortless, timeless and yours. Eight years later we still design every piece in-house, in small runs, with mills we know by name.

Craftsmanship & materials

Made slowly, by people we know by name

Every Chio piece is made by one of eleven family-run partners in Portugal, Italy and Spain. We visit each one every season, pay above the living wage benchmark, and choose fibres for how they age, not just how they look on day one.
