# Operator guide

## Link your Telegram account (once)

1. In Telegram, open a private chat with the site's bot and press **Start**.
2. In WordPress → Televixo → Setup (*Link my Telegram account*) or ask an admin for a code from Operators & teams.
3. Send `/link CODE` to the bot within 10 minutes. The bot confirms.

Do not post as "anonymous admin" in the group — anonymous messages cannot be attributed and are never delivered.

## Answering in Telegram

* **Reply Mode:** tap *Reply* on the visitor's message (any part of a long message works) and type. Replying to a colleague's answer also works. Messages that are not replies are not sent.
* **Topics Mode:** open the conversation's topic and write normally.
* Your first reply claims an unassigned conversation. If someone else already has it, the bot tells you and your text is not sent.
* Files and photos you send are delivered (up to 20 MB, allowed types only).
* `#note text` (or `/note text`) adds an internal note — never shown to the visitor.
* Commands: `/claim`, `/close`, `/spam`, `/online` (available for 8 h), `/away`, `/offline`. Anything starting with `/` is never sent to visitors.
* Buttons under new conversations: **Claim**, **Close**.

## Answering in WordPress

Televixo → Inbox. Filters: Active, Mine, Unassigned, Closed, Spam; search by name, email or reference. Type `/` in the composer to insert a canned reply; Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends. Use the *Internal note* tab for team-only notes. Your inbox replies are also posted to Telegram so the team sees them. Set **My availability** to *Available* while you work — visitors see "online" only while at least one operator is available and active.

## What the visitor sees

Your first name, your messages, "Seen" once they were read in the inbox or you replied, and a typing indicator when you type in the WordPress inbox (Telegram typing cannot be detected). "Delivered to the team" only means Telegram accepted their message.
