How it works
Televixo
Three steps. Zero friction.
1

Visitor writes on your site

A beautiful widget, no sign-up. Files, emoji, voice notes and pre-chat forms.

2

Your team gets it in Telegram

In a group with Reply Mode, or one forum topic per conversation.

3

The reply reaches the visitor

Instantly on the website — even across tabs and page reloads.

WordPress stays the source of truth. Messages, files, routing and delivery status live in your own database. Telegram is just the conversation channel.
