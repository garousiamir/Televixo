## Item description

**Your website chat, answered in Telegram.**

Televixo adds a polished chat widget to your WordPress site. Visitors write without signing up. Your team receives every conversation in a Telegram group — or answers from a full inbox inside WordPress. Everything is stored in your own database. No SaaS, no monthly fees.

### How it works

1. A visitor writes in the chat widget on your site.
2. Your team receives it in Telegram — as a message to reply to (Reply Mode) or in its own forum topic (Topics Mode).
3. The operator's reply appears instantly on the website.

### Highlights

* **Reply Mode & Topics Mode** — replies are matched to the exact conversation; operators are linked individually and unknown or anonymous senders are rejected.
* **WordPress inbox** — claim and transfer, teams, round-robin and least-busy routing, internal notes, tags, priority, canned replies, visitor context and history.
* **Targeting & automation** — visual rule builder with AND/OR groups, exclusions and frequency caps: pages, URL wildcards, WooCommerce cart and products, time on page, scroll depth, desktop exit intent, UTM, business hours. Welcome flows with hand-off to a human.
* **Complete chat experience** — pre-chat and offline forms, file uploads with server-side checks, emoji, voice notes (in supporting browsers), help articles, ratings, transcripts, sound and browser notifications.
* **Honest availability** — business hours, holidays and away mode; "online" is shown only when a real operator is available.
* **Reliable delivery** — messages are stored first and delivered through a durable queue with retries, Telegram rate-limit handling, ordering per conversation and a dead-letter screen.
* **Secure & private** — encrypted bot token, signed webhooks, rate limits, WordPress personal-data export and erase, retention settings. No tracking, no external fonts.
* **Fast & compatible** — 22 KB widget isolated from your theme's CSS, safe with full-page caching, works on shared hosting (no Node, Redis or WebSockets).
* **Global** — RTL support, translation ready (POT included), Persian translation of the visitor-facing text.
* **Reports** — first response, resolution time, queue wait, CSAT, operator and team performance, automation conversion.

### Requirements

WordPress 6.7+, PHP 8.2+, MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.4+, HTTPS, and a free Telegram bot created with @BotFather.

### Good to know

* Everyone in the Telegram group you choose can read relayed messages. Use a separate group per team, or the WordPress inbox, when conversations must stay private between teams.
* Telegram does not report operator online status or read receipts; Televixo never invents them.
