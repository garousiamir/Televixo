FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do my visitors need Telegram?

No. Visitors use the chat widget on your website. Only your operators use Telegram — or the WordPress inbox if they prefer.

Is there a monthly fee or external service?

No. Televixo is self-hosted. The only external service is the free Telegram Bot API, using your own bot.

What is the difference between Reply Mode and Topics Mode?

In Reply Mode, operators reply to the bot’s message in a group. In Topics Mode, every conversation gets its own forum topic and operators simply write inside it. You can switch at any time; existing conversations keep their routing.

Who can see the messages in Telegram?

Everyone in the Telegram group you choose. For private teams, use a separate group per team or answer in the WordPress inbox.

Will it work on shared hosting?

Yes. It needs PHP 8.2+, HTTPS and outbound access to Telegram. A real cron job is recommended for the fastest delivery on low-traffic sites.

Does it work with caching plugins and WooCommerce?

Page HTML contains nothing personal, so full-page caching is safe. WooCommerce targeting conditions (cart value, products, checkout pages) activate automatically when WooCommerce is installed.

Is it translation ready?

Yes. All strings are translatable, a POT file is included, and a Persian translation of the visitor-facing text ships with RTL support.
